# Finalize the ThystTV 1.5.0 candidate

## Goal

Combine the approved feature stack and two scoped follow-ups, prepare version
1.5.0/code 15 and build a verifiable signed release candidate. Publish only after
the exact candidate satisfies the documented review and device-approval gates.

## Non-goals

No broad player refactor, upstream sync, website redesign or account changes.
Do not claim physical Cast/TV QA or exact-signed-APK approval from debug testing.

## Decisions

- Ship Cast in the candidate labelled experimental, off by default. Hardware is
  unavailable, so its compatibility remains unverified. Keep-on-exit is separate
  and also defaults off.
- Preserve the build-once release pipeline. General phone testing cannot approve
  a later signed APK; do not tag or publish before exact-byte approval.
